dotz

A personal assistant that stays on, on your own computer.

It reads new mail, drafts the reply, and waits for you to press send from your phone.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ddotz/dotz/main/install.sh | sh
  1. Run wsl --install in PowerShell as administrator.
  2. Restart, then paste the command above into the Ubuntu window.
  3. dotz runs in WSL and works with Windows from there. Setup guide (Korean)

Try the demo Star on GitHub

The demo and the web app screens are in Korean for now.

What it does

  • Replies wait for one tap

    dotz reads new mail and drafts a reply in your tone. Press send if it looks right.

  • Ask from your phone

    Away from your desk, ask about today's schedule or your mail in the messenger you already use.

  • Catch the price drop

    Pick a product to watch, and your phone gets a notice when the price goes down.

Why dotz

  • Runs on your machine

    It uses the logins and files already on your computer. Secrets stay in ~/.dotz/secrets.

  • Nothing goes out without your OK

    Every mail, reply or post waits in the chat until you approve it.

  • One session per project

    Work stopped by a restart or a dropped connection starts again by itself.

  • Finds past conversations

    Ask for "that thing from last week". Indexing and search run on this computer only.

Works with

  • Discord
  • Web app (phone, PC)
  • Telegram
  • Slack
  • JANDI
  • Mail, calendar
  • YouTube, Instagram
  • Website comments
